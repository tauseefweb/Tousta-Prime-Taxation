US Business Tax Filing

Returns for LLCs, S-corps, C-corps, partnerships and sole proprietors.

Get startedPay online

I prepare returns for each type of U.S. business and make sure the right form is filed for how the business is structured: sole proprietorship, partnership, LLC, S-corporation or C-corporation.

What is included

  • Schedule C, Form 1065, Form 1120-S and Form 1120 returns
  • Federal and state business filings
  • Owner and shareholder reporting such as K-1s
  • Deductions, depreciation and year-end review
  • Reporting for foreign-owned and foreign-connected entities

Who it is for

  • Sole proprietors and freelancers
  • LLC, partnership and corporation owners
  • Online and service businesses

Documents to have ready

  • Profit and loss and balance sheet, or bookkeeping files
  • Business bank and card statements
  • Payroll reports and contractor payments
  • Prior-year business returns and EIN letter

Common questions

It depends on how the LLC is taxed. A single-member LLC is usually reported on the owner's return, a multi-member LLC generally files Form 1065, and an LLC can elect S or C corporation treatment. I confirm this at the start.
